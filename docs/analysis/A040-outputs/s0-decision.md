## Independent priority judgment

Among the documented alternatives reviewed, this task has the strongest current
case for a small, bounded investment: a distinct pretrained predictor could be
tested without reconstructing VAF routing, while current public code/weights and
probability interfaces make deployment questions answerable. It can yield a
concrete technical contract or eliminate the route cheaply. This is not a claim
that TabICLv2 is the best HSCC model or has a measured positive expected score.
No data support a numerical winning probability or Grand forecast.

The proposed mechanism is **GIVEN-only complete-answer probability prediction
with a frozen tabular in-context prior**, assessed against the actual E041 stack.
E041 already learns nonlinear predictions; nonlinear interactions alone are not
new. The potentially different component is the pretrained in-context estimator
and its usable context, rather than another retrieval metric or confidence gate.
Useful complementarity must eventually mean improved held-out final floored log
score under an attainable policy; prediction disagreement is insufficient.

Working family for the information task: direct distributions for UNHCR PREDICT
items, including the gate-sentinel class, using GIVEN features only. Investigate
the all213-output workload first, preserving UNICEF/WB. Keep original E041
available as the comparator/fallback; no newly learned respondent-level router.
Do not force schema gates onto the direct expert, change donor/threshold/seed
settings, or silently cut the workload to four parents to claim feasibility.
This is a study boundary, not an implemented model or empirical preregistration.
