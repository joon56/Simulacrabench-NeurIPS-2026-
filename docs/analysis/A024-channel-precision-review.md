## Prospective analysis skeleton — not an executable empirical registration

### Estimand and pairing

Keep A023's fixed legacy/upstream/endpoints library and P/D selection contracts.
Let c_P and c_D be choices made using S only, frozen before E. Both evaluation
predictions are the **deployed recursive output** of the selected candidate:
D(c_D) versus D(c_P). P's marginal/retrieval proxy chooses c_P; it is not the
probability representation to compare with D on final E. Exact R is the separate
original E041 procedure. If c_P=c_D and the shared artifacts are identical,
D-P is identically zero; that logical case is not an observed result here.

For each E respondent, average the paired realized log-probability differences
over all scored heads, with unchanged official normalization/floor. Primary
contrasts are delta_DP and delta_DR, positive favoring D. For the UNHCR contract
this includes all213 heads, not just active61/135 or selected descendants.
Equal head count makes the respondent mean equivalent to pooled cell mean;
other scoring/missingness designs require explicit weights fixed beforehand.
Translate to instrument skill only with that channel's fixed schema U. No
Grand forecast follows from an external proxy.

Use the actual independent sampling/group unit and design of the chosen channel.
SSUID is SIPP's conservative boundary, not an automatic unit for another survey.
All repeated/person/household links must stay within roles; country/wave/design
dependence needs an explicit analysis decision.213 cells from a person are not
213 independent observations. A single frozen training run supports inference
conditional on that fit and selection, not uncertainty across retraining,
dataset selection, humanitarian populations or changed deployment refits.

### Selection and two final claims

The existing S rule (.003 plus group2SE, strict guards, fixed order/fallback)
stays unchanged. It defines an algorithm; it is not a multiplicity-adjusted
efficacy test or proof of adequate power. Building all3 candidates before S and
locking c_P/c_D before an independent E prevents choosing a better mask on E.
Evaluating just those frozen choices does not require testing each candidate on
E. No extra heads, subgroups, masks or alternative outcomes may be promoted to
confirmatory claims after E. Any such future family must be declared beforehand.

A concrete conservative option for a future two-claim report is family error
alpha=.05 with Bonferroni allocation .025 to each contrast: two two-sided97.5%
intervals give at least95% simultaneous coverage **if** their component intervals
are valid under the chosen sampling assumptions. Shared respondents and shared
D induce dependence but do not invalidate the Bonferroni union bound. A future
cluster-t/bootstrap implementation and small/unequal-cluster treatment must be
specified and justified; nominal coverage is not exact finite-sample proof.
This is a prospective proposal, not a change to any earlier gate or an approved
empirical run. It covers two separately reported claims. A conjunction-only
intersection-union claim has different logic; do not silently switch between
that claim and two separate improvement claims after seeing results.

### Precision inputs that remain missing

The future protocol must select a smallest useful effect and/or maximum interval
half-width for EACH contrast, on a declared log-score/skill scale, based on the
decision it would change. The .003 S guard, historical+.005 adoption bar and
A0191000/500 counts do not supply those quantities. No numeric effect forecast,
minimum sample or power claim is invented in this review.

For an unweighted person-pooled mean with G independent groups, group sums S_g,
group sizes n_g and N people, A021's cluster estimate is
`SE^2 = G/(G-1) * sum_g (S_g - n_g*delta_hat)^2 / N^2`.
Counts alone provide none of the required group sums/difference variance.
Planning needs group-size/design information and justified variance/covariance
inputs or conservative sensitivity bounds. A target half-width h requires a
planned critical-value-times-SE no larger than h under the specified assumptions;
joint success/power for two contrasts also depends on their relationship, or
needs a conservative joint guarantee. Two individual power targets do not
automatically equal a joint power target.

Acceptable future inputs could be genuinely separate authorized pilot evidence,
relevant existing external summaries, explicitly labeled variance scenarios, or
a worst-case bound derived from the exact scorer and group weights. Their scope
and conservatism must be stated. Never open E to estimate nuisance quantities and
then retune the protocol or call E untouched. E059's fixed-parent leaf-family SE
cannot establish variance for A023's cross-section selection procedures; it is
not a justified numerical planning estimate merely because both use log scores.

If a final registered interval misses its desired precision, report inconclusive
under that registration; no automatic resampling, threshold change, seed search,
additional candidate or audit-driven revision. Whether the final decision needs
both lower bounds above zero, a practical delta, or a separate noninferiority
margin must be settled before evaluation; no existing STOP is reinterpreted.
