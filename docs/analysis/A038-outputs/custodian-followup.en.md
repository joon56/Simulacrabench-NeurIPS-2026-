Subject: Clarification of v2.1 export keys and missing-value coding — Jordan VAF Host 2023

Dear UNHCR Data Curation Team,

Thank you for approving my access. I have downloaded
UNHCR_JOR_2023_VAF_HOST_v2.1 and its accompanying documentation. Before linking
the released tables, I would appreciate clarification of the following points.
A reference to existing release-specific documentation would be very helpful.

1. **Exported identifiers and linkage.** The researcher guide and DDI describe
   householdindex, sharingindex, familyindex, caseindex and individualindex,
   together with Form. The downloaded variable-label tables instead list ID_main
   in all six tables, and ID_ind in the member table. What units do these identifiers
   identify, and how do they map to the documented hierarchy? Which released keys
   safely link main, case, family, member and sharing-group records? In particular,
   can a member be assigned to a specific family/case/sharing group, or is only
   household-level linkage retained? Can the same household appear under different
   IDs or in both long and short forms?

2. **Routing and missing values.** For v2.1, how are questions not asked due to
   routing, interview breakoff, refusal, don't know, ordinary missing values and
   edited/imputed values represented? Are distinguishing codes or flags retained?
   If some states cannot be distinguished in the released files, confirmation of
   that limitation would be equally useful. We do not want to treat every blank
   as a routing skip.

3. **Questionnaire-to-release mapping.** Is there a crosswalk or cleaning note
   linking the supplied long/short KOBO questionnaires to the v2.1 variable names,
   category codes and retained routing information? We are trying to distinguish
   original routing from changes made during editing/anonymisation, rather than
   assuming the questionnaire alone describes all released values.

My research concerns probabilistic prediction of survey responses and may inform
participation in the Humanitarian Survey Completion Competition / SimulacraBench,
which offers prizes. Please also point me to the terms applicable to my approved
request if these are not already included in the approval materials. In particular,
I would like to distinguish local methodological evaluation from any later use or
distribution of trained models through a hosted competition. I am not requesting
respondent records by email or proposing redistribution of the raw microdata.

Thank you for your help.

Best regards,
Minjoon Yoo
