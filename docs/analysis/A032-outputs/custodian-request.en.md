Proposed recipient: UNHCR Microdata Library, microdata@unhcr.org

Subject: HOST VAF2023 v2.1 — release coding, linkage and permitted research use

Dear UNHCR Microdata Library team,

I am preparing a methodological study of probabilistic completion of humanitarian
survey questions, including skip routing and non-response. I am considering the
Jordan Host Communities VAF release UNHCR_JOR_2023_VAF_HOST_v2.1, catalog1030,
as an external research dataset. No individual-level data from this release have
been downloaded for this review, and I am not requesting any identifiable data.

I located the catalog-linked VAF_Host_KOBO questionnaire archive, researcher
guide and DDI metadata. The XLSForm settings list long version2310162710 and
short version2312062333. Could you identify any additional release-specific
documentation covering the following points?

1. Which deployed questionnaire versions and transformations produced v2.1?
   Is there an export/cleaning crosswalk from XLSForm names and choices to each
   released file's variables and categorical codes?
2. How can users distinguish questions never asked because of routing, interview
   breakoff, refusal, don't-know, other missing values and edited/imputed values?
   Are corresponding flags retained? If these states cannot be distinguished,
   confirmation of that limitation would also be useful.
3. Is there documentation of routing exceptions or disagreement remaining after
   editing? A description of known limitations would suffice; no respondent
   records or private diagnostics are requested.
4. The guide specifies Form plus the relevant index for linking files. Do those
   combinations identify the same household consistently across released levels?
   Can the same underlying household occur across long/short forms or repeated
   records? What non-identifying linkage/sampling information should guide
   household-disjoint research splits? No linkage to identify people is proposed.
5. What application and approval are required for this release? The possible
   research would evaluate or train probabilistic models and inform participation
   in the Humanitarian Survey Completion Competition / SimulacraBench, which has
   prizes. Please distinguish permission for local evaluation, model training,
   use of derived models in hosted competition submissions, publication of
   aggregate research results, and sharing derived weights or code. Raw microdata
   would not be redistributed. Please identify applicable team/processing-host,
   review, disclosure, citation and security requirements.

This is an inquiry about documentation and the appropriate approval route, not
an assertion that these uses are already authorized. Researcher/team identity,
institutional authority and processing environment would be supplied by the
responsible applicant through the official process.

Thank you.
