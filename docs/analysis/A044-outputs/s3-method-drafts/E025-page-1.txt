 1. Task, evidence and schema handling
 We predict categorical survey answers from each held-out respondent's GIVEN block for UNICEF Faith &
 Immunisation, World Bank Skills Assessment and UNHCR ERPIS. Every PREDICT cell is blanked and scored;
 EXCLUDE items are omitted. One self-contained predictor learns separately per instrument using complete visible
 respondents, combining hierarchical smoothing, a shared neural network, approximate skip logic and similar-row
 donor evidence. No fitted state is bundled.
 E025 is a preserved alternative to provisional E041, not a user-selected final TEST model. Its historical user-reported
 DEV readings were Grand 0.63, UNICEF 0.52, World Bank 0.49 and UNHCR 0.89. E041's later released API record
 ID933094 reports 0.6433333333/0.52/0.49/0.92 (Grand displayed as 0.64). These retained readings remain subject to
 phase-1 Laplace noise and 0.01 rounding. Extra serialized decimals do not establish precise effects. They compare
 adaptively developed complete pipelines, not independent component trials. No real TEST score exists; E064 is
 parked awaiting DEV delivery, with no actual hosted result. [6,7]
 Phase 1 shows TRAIN and scores DEV; phase 2 shows TRAIN+DEV and scores TEST. The pinned kit describes
 shuffled respondent roles, stratified by country for UNICEF and balanced by survey round/country for UNHCR, with
 ordinary permutation for World Bank. This is not a chronological or new-country holdout. Same-population split
 relevance does not guarantee refit gain or repair adaptive DEV selection. [1,6]
 The official metric averages log probabilities over all scored cells within an instrument, then scales by schema-only
 uniform surprisal U. Grand is the equally weighted mean of the three instrument skills. K includes the sentinel for
 gated items. Confident errors are costly.

 p'_k = 0.001 + (1 - K*0.001) * p_k / sum_j(p_j)
 skill = 1 + mean_cells(log p'_[true answer]) / U
 U = mean_scored_items(log K_i)
 Vectors follow schema item/option order, adding NA_GATED as the last option for gated items. NaN means predict;
 refusal and other non-response categories remain ordinary answers. Decoding passes parents' final beliefs, including
 donor evidence, to descendants. A visible parent gives a one-hot belief. For gated item  i, r_i is parent mass on
 observed-if values.

 hard_i = [r_i * asked_conditional_i, 1 - r_i]
 base_i = a_i(x) * hard_i + (1 - a_i(x)) * full_i
 Asked-conditional heads train on asked rows; unrestricted full heads include the sentinel. UNHCR gate agreement is
 approximate (median 0.83, range 0.29-0.98), so hard coupling can be wrong even with a known parent. Scalar
 agreement (k_i+20*m)/(n_i+20) shrinks toward median observed agreement m, not one. For each child, an internal
 20% split can select one GIVEN column of cardinality at most 12; its value-specific rates shrink toward the scalar.
 Without improvement the scalar remains. [1,6]
