 2. Runtime prediction, donor matching and replay
 Hierarchical backoff. A smoothed global marginal anchors a ladder, with each level adding a GIVEN column.
 Column order, depth up to three and kappa in {1,3,10,30,100} are selected greedily by held-out log score on 20% of
 complete visible rows. Candidate columns have at most 12 levels. Asked-only distributions use the same ladder;
 sparse cells borrow strength and unseen cells back off.

 p_L(v | cell) = (n_L(v) + kappa_L*p_(L-1)(v)) / (N_L+kappa_L)
 Shared trunk. One-hot GIVEN inputs feed a Linear-ReLU network with softmax full heads for PREDICT items and
 separate asked-only heads for gated items. Masked cross-entropy pools observed full and conditional targets.
 Full-batch Adam uses learning rate 0.003, weight decay 0.0001, at most 500 epochs and patience 30 on an inner split.
 An outer split chooses two 64-unit layers/no dropout or two 128-unit layers/dropout 0.1. The selected epoch count is
  refitted on all complete visible rows. One instrument-level network/ladder mixing weight from {0,0.1,...,1} is
 selected on the outer split and shared by full and conditional heads. Fixed split and network seeds are zero.
 Visible-row evidence. Every complete visible respondent is a potential donor, without a cross-round restriction.
 Matching uses GIVEN values, never respondent identifiers. Initial weights are -log(u_j), where u_j is random
 agreement estimated from available GIVEN frequencies, including held-out GIVEN covariates. That covariate use
 does not include held-out PREDICT responses.
 A preliminary match identifies provisional pairs with top posterior mass at least 0.5. With at least 30 such pairs, one
 Fellegi-Sunter-style update estimates per-column agreement m_j; otherwise the rarity weights remain. Weighted
 GIVEN agreement is normalized with softmax over the top ten donors, whose answer vectors supply item evidence.

 weight_j = max(0, log(m_j/u_j) - log((1-m_j)/(1-u_j)))
 This soft neighborhood can exploit ordinary similarity or near-duplicate structure; it does not establish household
 relationships or identities. An organizer ruling retained in the project allows matching within the supplied dataset
 without external identity linkage. We do not assert newly authenticated organizer correspondence or link to external
 records. [6]
 Concentration-specific calibration. Self-excluded replay uses up to 6,000 complete visible queries. For each item,
 lambda is chosen from {0,0.1,...,0.9,0.95,0.98}, separately when maximum donor mass is below or at least 0.5. Each
 bucket requires 30 valid observations; insufficient support leaves lambda zero. The query is excluded as its own
 donor. The final update follows gate mixing and precedes descendants' decoding.

 final_i = (1-lambda_i) * base_i + lambda_i * evidence_i
 No positive learned lambda means base-only prediction. No usable GIVEN signature supplies no evidence. GPU
 training exceptions can use CPU fallback; arbitrary whole-run failures and timeouts do not create a new TEST
 attempt. E025 has no E041 learned section metric, 32-candidate reranking, metric-fit/selection/replay partition
 or section activation gate. Its two-architecture choice, top-ten donors, replay cap and lambda grid are unchanged.
