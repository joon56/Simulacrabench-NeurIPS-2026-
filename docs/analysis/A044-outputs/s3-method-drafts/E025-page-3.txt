 3. Calibration limits, development evidence and phase-2 refit
 Ladder selection, conditional agreement, architecture/epoch choice and network mixing use internal visible-row
  splits. Donor replay excludes self-matches, but base predictions are refitted on all visible rows, including replay
  labels.  Self-exclusion  therefore does  not  cross-fit  calibration, and  replay performance  is  not an unbiased
 generalization estimate. No E041 partitioning is implicitly credited to E025, and no repair of this optimism is
 claimed.
 Algorithm choices and grids were developed through synthetic work, public instrument descriptions and repeated
 DEV feedback. Parameters are estimated at runtime, but the retained method was selected adaptively during
 development. Legal TRAIN+DEV final fitting does not make those DEV observations independent again. Fixed
 seeds do not prevent row-order, CPU/GPU or reduction-related numerical variation. [6]
 Complete phase-2 visible counts grow to 16,373 UNICEF, 5,715 World Bank and 8,900 UNHCR respondents. Every
  fitted component  is  rebuilt from  that frame: adding DEV can change marginals, asked-only heads, ladder
 order/shrinkage, network architecture/epochs/mixing, gate coefficients, agreement weights and replay lambdas. The
 larger visible bank may help or hurt; no linear runtime or gain forecast follows. Larger replay caps, ensembles,
  identifier signatures, tree blends and relation-specific evidence variants are not part of this predictor.
 Development counterevidence. Six later hosted comparisons were flat at displayed resolution: evidence as network
 input (E027), parent-value gate decoding (E028), larger replay (E031b), relation-specific weights (E036), identifier
